Al Ain, United Arab Emirates. 12 hectares, brackish groundwater, a fixed water quota.

A scientist for the desert farm.

WorldLab plants trial plots, learns how the local crops really behave, trains its own irrigation policy, and catches what goes wrong in the field. You choose the crops for each season. It runs the farm and shows its working.

How a season runs

The scientist never sees the true crop traits or the faults we hide in the field. It works from sensors, drone images, harvests and tests, the way an agronomist would.

  1. Trial season

    Trial plots

    Sixteen small plots, each with a predicted yield range written down before planting. A saline plot exposes salt tolerance; a late sowing exposes heat at flowering.

  2. After the trials

    Calibrate

    Missed predictions update its beliefs about four hidden traits per crop, and only for traits a plot actually tested.

  3. Before sowing

    Plan for a bad year

    It chooses sowing day, irrigation, water blend and nitrogen for every field, maximising the people fed in the worst tenth of years, within the water and energy limits.

  4. Before sowing

    Learn an irrigation policy

    Reinforcement learning inside its own model of the farm: about 40,000 imagined seasons in a minute. It uses the policy only if it beats the fixed rule.

  5. October to June

    Run and check

    Every day its model runs beside the farm. When a field drifts out of the predicted band, it stops, tests competing explanations, repairs the cause and corrects itself.

Ten crops, three sowing seasons

The growing year runs from October to June. Pick a season for each field and the crops that can be sown in it; the scientist plans everything else and tells you how your choice compares with its own.

You pick, it plans

For each of the six fields you can choose a crop and its sowing season, leave the field fallow, or let the scientist decide. Each crop card shows its sowing window, days to harvest, what it covers (calories, protein or vitamin A) and the yield the model expects on every field.

A person counts as fed only when all three needs are met, so a farm of wheat alone feeds nobody. The planner keeps your crops and balances the rest.

Tested on 25 farms with hidden faults

Each test farm has its own hidden crop traits, drip systems and weather year. On day 90 we clog a drip line, release pests, salt the groundwater or send a heat wave, without telling the scientist.

Built on Meta's models

The language model decides; numerical code does the agronomy; budgets bound every action. Every decision and its reason goes into a hash-chained ledger anyone can verify.
